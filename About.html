About Us

The IKEA Catalogue Archive serves as a comprehensive digital retrospective, meticulously documenting every seasonal catalogue released by IKEA from 1950 to 2021. For over seven decades, these pages have done more than just list prices; they have captured the evolving heart of the modern home. Our mission is to aggregate these historical snapshots into a single, accessible gallery. By bringing these archives together, we aim to showcase the dramatic shifts in global design aesthetics, interior functionality, and cultural trends over time. From the mid-century minimalism of the fifties to the bold, sustainable innovations of the 2020s, we invite you to explore the visual history of how we live.
